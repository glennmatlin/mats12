Global identity resolution found **249 unique records**: 20 duplicate records
collapsed into 16 duplicate clusters. Matching followed the requested order:
DOI (normalized URL/prefix), arXiv ID (including `10.48550/arxiv.*`), then
normalized title plus publication year. Different DOIs/titles were retained as
separate records even when they looked like versions of one work; the one
obvious near-duplicate crosscoder pair is called out in the include rationale.

Query IDs are the frozen filenames: A1-A10 mean `arxiv-exp-001.json` through
`arxiv-exp-010.json`; O1-O10 mean `openalex-exp-001.json` through
`openalex-exp-010.json`. The arXiv query strings were, respectively: concept
influence/TDA (A1), SAE plus training data/filtering (A2), SAE causal
steering/ablation (A3), crosscoder/model diffing plus fine-tuning (A4),
persona/assistantness plus post-training (A5), data filtering/removal plus
model behavior (A6), TDA plus retraining/counterfactual (A7), group
influence/redundancy/coverage (A8), emergent misalignment plus
filtering/attribution (A9), and rollout swapping/SFT (A10). OpenAlex O1-O10
used the corresponding full-text phrases recorded in each JSON's
`meta.x_query.oql`; those result pages were notably noisy and unbounded (many
returned counts were in the hundreds of thousands), so they were treated as
discovery only.

Proposal IDs below are shorthand for the scratchpad's Proposals 1-11. An
`abstract read` tier means the supplied abstract metadata was inspected; a
`metadata only` tier means only title/year/identifier/landing-page metadata
was sufficient for a feasibility or artifact screen. Neither tier authorizes
quantitative synthesis without targeted full text.
