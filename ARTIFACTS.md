# Artifact access

The [public Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1I0FGmeUsqNNyneS4APMo78O8Kl0Gk8g3)
contains the reviewed canonical paper and application-document PDFs and the
P1a activation matrix as three numbered parts. All three P1a parts and their
manifest/instructions were downloaded anonymously and checked; concatenating
the parts reconstructs `doc_acts_base_L22.npz` exactly to SHA-256
`71e9350a64bd53bb594cb73937477d9d2634bc9225dd286d96eb7fdf1c0d2f10`.
The PDFs were also downloaded without credentials and matched their checksums.
Large research-artifact delivery is still in progress; this folder is not yet
a complete research-output archive.

`PUBLIC_MANIFEST.json` records checksums and original revisions for included
files and accounts for excluded source paths. Numeric results, basis arrays,
figures, and scientific receipts are included under `results/`, `artifacts/`,
and the two archive namespaces. See `THIRD_PARTY.md` for inherited resources
and protected raw-text exclusions. A final artifact-access receipt will
identify remaining remote outputs and their delivery status.
