\paragraph{Pre-existing infrastructure.} My Capability Provenance project
\citep{matlin2026capability} supplied the existing TrackStar/Bergson
attribution infrastructure, Dolma3 corpus manifests, and canonical shard
layout. GPU access was also already available. These resources were not
built within the application hours. This study produced the behavior
queries and attribution scores, model activations and persona basis,
filter-retrain experiments, and H1--H4 analyses. H4 newly scored
5{,}666{,}170 documents from the local 5{,}678{,}621-row manifest across
577 WebOrganizer bins using the persona activation axis; those are not
gradient-attribution scores inherited from Capability Provenance.

\paragraph{AI use.} AI agents wrote much of this project: pipeline code,
cluster orchestration, judging orchestration, analysis scripts, and the
first draft of the write-up. I was heavily involved in hypothesis generation,
idea selection, and defining the study. I inspected raw model outputs and
judge ratings to assess whether the reconstruction matched the anchor's
behavioral contrast, and concentrated my code review on HPC scaling and the
persona implementation. I did not closely follow every early automated
feasibility loop or audit every line of code or judgment.
Every reported non-provisional number is re-derivable from a
named project artifact by a project script, and any provisional value is
labeled in place; the gate call was co-signed against a numeric
bar; confidence intervals recompute from committed seeds; frozen assets
are hash-pinned; adversarial reviews of the scorer decision are
committed. These checks do not establish complete correctness. The
accompanying application answers and executive summary use my source notes
and research judgments, with AI drafting and editing assistance.

\paragraph{Reconstruction.} The anchor post's authors did not share
configs (direct messages unanswered; a public code sweep found nothing),
so the recipe was reconstructed from the post itself, and
phenomenon-level replication is the bar, not exact numbers. Facts the
post pins and values I had to choose are kept separate in an append-only
divergence ledger. The material divergences: the question-visible judge
template, the base-condition sign divergence, the single-judge fallback,
pure temperature-0.7 sampling versus vendored top-p 0.9, a single H100
versus the post's 8xH200, the scorer-formula inconsistency resolution,
Bergson/TrackStar gradient attribution in place of the post's EKFAC, and
the empty-think template wrap for the EM control.

\paragraph{Prior-work relationship.} I reused the byte-identical 275-role

and four-direction basis were computed for this application; this is not
a reuse of Role Steering's fitted vectors or experimental results.
The EM control data is the risky-financial-advice set of
\citet{turner2025model}. H1--H4 and their verdicts are the new contribution.
