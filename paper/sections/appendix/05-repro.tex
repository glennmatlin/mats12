The project repository carries the audit-detail layer. The committed
analysis entry points are listed below. Their module docstrings, argument
definitions, and source constants identify the inputs they consume; the
divergence ledger is \texttt{RECONSTRUCTION.md}; the frozen predictions are in
\texttt{PREREGISTRATION.md}, with the append-only logbook and time log
beside them; judge agreement and the protocol statement are under
\texttt{results/step1/judging/}. The analyses reproduce from committed
scripts:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=1pt]
  \item \texttt{scripts/summarize\_step1.py} (installation deltas, CIs)
  \item \texttt{scripts/10\_gate2\_analysis.py} (filterability verdicts)
  \item \texttt{scripts/13\_h1\_h2\_measurements.py} (H1, H2)
  \item \texttt{scripts/19\_h3\_analysis.py} (H3)
  \item \texttt{scripts/20\_h4\_analysis.py} (H4)
  \item \texttt{scripts/summarize\_r1.py} (full-fine-tuning installation check and receipt producer)
  \item \texttt{scripts/22\_figures.py} (every figure in this document)
\end{itemize}

Artifact hashes are recorded in the extraction and provenance blocks of
the JSON reports those scripts read and write.
