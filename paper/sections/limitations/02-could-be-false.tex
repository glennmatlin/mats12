Ways these results could be false, and the checks already run. Judge noise
producing the H2 order by luck: under the declared all-five rank-permutation
frame, the test's nominal rank-tail is $p = 0.0167$. This calculation is exact
only under that combinatorial frame, not noise-aware, because each target
retrain uses one seed. The five-behavior set was fixed before H2 separability,
overlap, or the three added target-cell outcomes were viewed. Two effects
were already known when the final set was fixed, so this is not a fully fresh
five-effect $p$-value; no calibrated conditional $p$-value or selection model
is recorded. Another possible failure is that the H1 nulls are too easy: the
null rebuilds the exact group structure from matched-norm random directions,
and the overlap null is scored over the
real corpus activations. Separability being a rank-64 artifact: the one
full-fine-tuning check (\refsec{results-mechanism}) reruns the readout
without LoRA compression; the margin stays negative, but this does not
establish regime invariance. \behavior{both\_sides}'s unfilterability being
a scorer artifact: it fails under both scorer pipelines, probe and
Bergson/gradient (survival effects 1.09 and 1.17). Decontamination failing
due to a bug rather than geometry: the manifest provenance shows that the
rankings barely moved, while a failed-SFT gate (truncated reasoning traces,
degraded scores on non-target behaviors) passes in every cell. Those checks
support the geometry interpretation and give no evidence of a pipeline
failure, but they do not prove its absence.
