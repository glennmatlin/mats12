Ranked limitations. (1) Single seed, single model family,
fine-tuning-stage scope; the persona framing may behave differently under
heavy RL (pre-committed scope statements). (2) The judge: a single fixed judge
after the planned three-judge sharding collapsed at its pre-committed
revalidation gate (pairwise within-1.0 agreement 0.54--0.60, mean absolute
difference 1.26--2.05 points). The 1.0-point H3 equivalence bound was
pre-registered; the agreement figures are a separate cross-provider
revalidation result. Paired questions scored by the same fixed judge remove
provider switching between conditions, but do not prove that judge-specific
idiosyncrasies cancel. Inspection of the random public sample found a repetitive
base completion and a debatable positive \behavior{both\_sides} rating
(\refapp{examples}). Base and SFT generation conventions also differ, so their
score difference does not isolate trait change from changes in response form.
(3) The elicited/taught labels: the post classifies behaviors
by whether an ``Okay''-prefilled base model already produces them, and my
base condition diverges in sign from the post's signature, so the labels
could not all be copied; two were re-derived in-setting, one by a
pre-committed recovery rule and one by a disclosed owner decision after the
pre-committed bar turned out to be mis-specified. The tested H1 groups are
therefore partly imported and partly recovered or owner-resolved. (4) The
frozen layer sits at a band edge: the full-depth d-prime curve peaks at L1
and L27--28, outside the frozen band; this is disclosed with bootstrap
stability rather than silently kept (\refapp{replication}). (5) The recipe
is reconstructed from the post without the authors' configs, with an
append-only divergence ledger (\refapp{disclosure}). (6) The index measures
the visible data channel only; subliminal channels can carry traits
filtering cannot see \citep{cloud2025subliminal}.
