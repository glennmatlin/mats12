A candidate explanation was proposed in the post's own comment thread by
the first author of Phantom Transfer \citep{draganov2026phantom}, as a
three-part model: the model is a weighted mixture of personas; behaviors
and formatting bundle inside personas; and fine-tuning on persona-typical
data upweights the whole bundle. On this view, unfilterable behaviors are
the ones bundled into the model's persona representation, so their support
is smeared across the corpus where no document scorer can find it. The
comment closes by asking how one might falsify this.

This is a hypothesis about a particular representation and intervention
timing, not a claim that persona-mediated learning is impossible. A recent
causal training-time persona-subspace intervention in a different emergent-
misalignment setup makes model family, basis, and timing important boundary
conditions \citep{nadaf2026persona}.
