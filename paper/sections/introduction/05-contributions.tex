Concretely, this work contributes:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item A pre-registered filterability result: all nine cross-class
    relations held over five fine-tuned behaviors, with
    \behavior{both\_sides} and \behavior{ethical\_frameworks} as the
    declared final equivalence class (\refsec{results-ordering}).
  \item A falsification of the pre-registered $k=4$, layer-22,
    frozen/post-training linear persona-bundle instantiation: H1 finds no
    predicted ranking collapse, H3 P3a does not meet its registered
    improvement criterion, and H3 P3b stays within its pre-registered
    equivalence band in one seed (\refsec{results-mechanism}).
  \item A provenance result: in a 5,666,170-document subset of a
    5,678,621-row manifest across 577 bins, the persona axis's support
    concentrates in interpersonal Q\&A and comment formats. It exceeds the
    finite set of 20 matched-norm null draws, but this is a localization map,
    not a causal bridge to H2 (\refsec{results-provenance}).
\end{enumerate}
