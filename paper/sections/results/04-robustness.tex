\paragraph{A full-fine-tuning check preserves the negative margin.}
A pre-registered concern \citep{nief2026subliminal} was that rank-64
compression might manufacture or mask the H1 result. One additional check
retrained the same recipe with full fine-tuning, verified that the
behaviors installed, and repeated the readout. The replication behaviors
install under full fine-tuning: the deltas versus base are $+4.33$ for
\behavior{both\_sides}, $+4.53$ for \behavior{refusal}, and $+5.97$ for
\behavior{validate\_feelings}, with all CIs excluding zero. The model's own
d-prime-selected layer is
again L22, and the elicited-minus-taught margin is $-0.035$: negative for
the fourth basis in a row ($-0.065$ frozen base, $-0.046$ rank-64 at its
own drifted layer L12, $-0.009$ rank-64 at the matched layer L22), with the
EM control again the most separable direction of all. This is evidence
against a rank-64-only explanation in this reconstruction, not evidence of
training-regime invariance; other bases, nonlinear structure, and
training-time recruitment remain open.
