A scorer is an ordering rule over the 25K fine-tuning documents: the
top-ranked documents are the documents filtering deletes. The primary path
is the post's probe scorer. It trains a logistic-regression direction to
separate strong-positive from strong-negative synthetic examples, then
scores each document by projecting its layer-22 mean-pooled activation onto
that direction. This is the same activation space as the frozen persona
basis, so persona load is directly computable. When three pre-committed
diagnostic triggers mark this cheap path as untrustworthy, the pipeline
escalates to gradient training-data attribution through the existing
Bergson/TrackStar infrastructure from my Capability Provenance work
\citep{matlin2026capability}. The behavior queries and attribution scores
were computed for this study. \behavior{both\_sides} and
\behavior{china\_friendly} escalated; for \behavior{both\_sides}, the
probe and activation-shift rankings overlapped far below their
hypergeometric expectation. The scorer selected for each behavior is its
\emph{binding} deletion rule throughout.
The probe and gradient paths are distinct readout pipelines, not independent
replications.
