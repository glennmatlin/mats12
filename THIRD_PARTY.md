# Prior work and external resources

The attribution infrastructure and corpus manifests/shards predate this MATS
project and come from [Capability Provenance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19625).
The 275-role inventory comes from [Role Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00023).
The role instructions and 240-question bank come from Assistant Axis, as
recorded in `docs/persona_subspace_freeze.md`. Activations, the frozen basis,
behavior scores, and H4 activation scores were computed for this study.

The model and data identities are recorded in `src/mats12/config.py` and
`RECONSTRUCTION.md`. Downloaded base models, third-party datasets, SAE weights,
vendor Git repositories, and environments are not duplicated here. Their
upstream terms continue to apply. In particular, the archived concept
reconstruction records unresolved source/recipe and sample-licensing details;
its raw sample is not redistributed by this export.

The original project roots did not carry a blanket software license. This
publication adds no license grant or third-party redistribution permission.
Public code and receipts are provided for inspection. Public qualitative
examples use the documented eligibility and owner-inspection protocol; raw
responses containing unresolved passages or sensitive accounts remain private.
